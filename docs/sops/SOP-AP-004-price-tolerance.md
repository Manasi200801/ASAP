# SOP-AP-004 — Purchase Price Variance Tolerance

**Owner:** Accounts Payable, Finance Shared Services
**Applies to:** All supplier invoices matched to a purchase order, company code 1010
**Supersedes:** SOP-AP-004 rev. 2
**Effective:** 1 January 2025

## 1. Purpose

This procedure states when Accounts Payable may accept a supplier invoice whose unit
price differs from the price on the referenced purchase order line, and when the
invoice must instead be blocked and returned to Procurement.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the unit price comparison only. Quantity variances are governed by
SOP-AP-006. Tax determination is governed by SOP-AP-011.

## 3. Tolerance tiers

The variance is calculated on the **unit price**, as a percentage over the purchase
order price:

    variance % = (invoice unit price - PO unit price) / PO unit price x 100

| Tier | Variance over PO price | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| A | 0.00% to 5.00% | Accept. No approval required. |
| B | above 5.00% up to 10.00% | Accept **only** if the purchase order was raised more than 60 days before the invoice date. Otherwise block. |
| C | above 10.00% | Block. Return to Procurement for a purchase order amendment. |

Tier B exists because commodity and freight prices drift between the raising of a
long-lived order and its delivery. A recent order carries no such excuse, so the same
variance is not acceptable on it.

## 4. Absolute cap

Irrespective of tier, block the invoice where the variance exceeds **EUR 250.00 on a
single line**. A small percentage on a high-value line is still a material amount and
requires a purchase order amendment.

## 5. Invoices priced below the purchase order

A unit price **below** the purchase order price is always acceptable and is never a
reason to block. Post it at the invoiced amount. Do not correct it upward to match the
order.

## 6. Evidence

Record, against each invoice: the purchase order price, the invoiced price, the
computed variance percentage, the tier applied, and — where tier B was applied — the
age of the purchase order in days.
